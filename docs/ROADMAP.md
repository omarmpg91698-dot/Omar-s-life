## Phase 5: Wealth & Commitments
- Integer-minor financial ledger, category analytics, and Subscription Tracker with amortized monthly cost and renewal automations.

## Phase 6: People, Home & External Signals
- Personal CRM touchpoint cadence, Career Architect milestones, Home Manager maintenance & warranty logs, BI personal KPIs, and free CORS-safe Market RSS feeds.

## Phase 7: Optional Cloud Synchronization
- Optional Supabase Postgres + Auth + RLS delta sync using `sync_outbox` and `conflict_copies` while preserving full offline independence.
